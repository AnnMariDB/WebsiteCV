Professional profile

Profile

Data Engineer and Database Administrator with a Bachelor of Computing focused on Data Science, Business Intelligence and Data Analytics, and Microsoft Certified in Azure Data Fundamentals (DP-900). Main contact for a multi-instance SQL Server support account, where I rebuilt the daily Availability Group and server health reports, resolved Availability Group synchronisation issues, led a SQL Agent job and database ownership review, applied cumulative updates under change control and present the monthly health review to the client. Designed and built the internal Goals & Growth reporting platform end to end and delivered a multi-page Power BI dashboard used internally as a client demonstration.

Particularly passionate about Business Intelligence and Data Analytics, especially Power BI dashboards.
